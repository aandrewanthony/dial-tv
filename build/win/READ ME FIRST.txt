DIAL TV FOR WINDOWS
===================

1. Right-click the zip you downloaded and choose "Extract All..." (don't run it from inside the zip).
2. Open the extracted folder and double-click "Dial TV".
3. Click "Install". Dial TV installs itself for your account with Start menu and desktop
   shortcuts, then opens. You can delete the zip and extracted folder afterwards.

Updating: download the new zip and do the same. "Update" keeps your settings, playlists and picks.
Uninstalling: Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps > Dial TV > Uninstall.

If Windows SmartScreen shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info" > "Run anyway".
